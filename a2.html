Australian University Enrolments 2024

As a country, Australia emphasises education beyond high school whether that be in university, TAFEs, or other specialised courses. The country boasts over 40 universities with a handful being internationally recognised, attracting many students from across the globe.

Every year, around one-third of students enrolled in Australian Universities is made up of international students. This emphasises the importance of education as an export for the Australian economy.

While there hasn't been a significant change in the number of Australians enrolled in university (due to somewhat stagnant population growth), the number of foreign students enroling has been increasing since 2021 when Australia reopened its borders.

International Student Origins

In 2024, the majority of enrolled international students were born in India or China. Other notable countries include New Zealand and The Philippines.

This geographical distribution reflects Australia's strategic position in the Asia-Pacific region and its strong educational ties with neighboring countries.

Student Enrolment Choices

The charts in this section look further into enrolment patterns by area of study and institute choices.

From these charts we can see that Victoria and NSW have the most enrolments and institutions in Australia, and around 50% of adult teenagers in Australia are enroled in university.

These are great statistics for Australia as it means we are creating an educated population faster.

Popular Areas of Study

In 2024, Management and Commerce was by far the most popular field of study, with enrolments significantly exceeding all other faculties. The data reveals a substantial gap between the top three faculties and the remaining fields, with Information Technology, Education, and Natural Sciences forming a middle tier, while more specialized fields like Agriculture, Architecture, and Food/Hospitality have notably lower enrolment numbers.
