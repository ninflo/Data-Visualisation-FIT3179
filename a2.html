Australian University Enrolments 2024

As a country, Australia emphasises education beyond high school whether that be at university, TAFE, or other specialised courses. The country boasts over 40 universities with a handful being internationally recognised, attracting many students from across the globe.

Every year, around one-third of students enrolled in Australian Universities is made up of international students. This emphasises the importance of education as an export for the Australian economy.

While there hasn't been a significant change in the number of Australians enrolled in university (due to somewhat stagnant population growth), the number of foreign students enroling has been increasing since 2021 when Australia reopened its borders (post COVID-19 outbreak).

International Student Origins

In 2024, the majority of enrolled international students were born in India or China. Other notable countries include New Zealand and The Philippines.

This geographical distribution reflects Australia's strategic position in the Asia-Pacific region and its strong educational ties with neighboring countries.

Popular Areas of Study

In 2024, Management and Commerce was by far the most popular field of study. The substantial gap between the top three faculties and the remaining fields is likely due to those areas of study being quite broad as well as the huge demand for healthcare workers. In general it seems that the more popular areas of study are ones that are highly applicable to gaps in the current Australian labour force.
The more specialized areas like Agriculture, Architecture, and Food/Hospitality have very low enrolment numbers compared to other fields likely due to being less in demand.
